Natya Lab

Natya Lab is an interactive learning portal that helps dancers discover Bharatanatyam through hastas (hand gestures) and rasas (emotions). Through real-time feedback, storytelling, and guided exploration, learners move beyond memorisation to develop deeper understanding, creativity, and confidence.

Current Method

For generations, Bharatanatyam has been taught through observation, repetition, and correction.

Students learn a gesture

They memorise its meaning

They repeat it until its right

They may / may not remember what they memorised

While this preserves tradition, it often leaves little room to explore how gestures and emotions can become tools for personal expression.

About Natya Lab

By combining movement recognition, visual feedback, and storytelling exercises,
Natya Lab helps learners understand not only how a gesture is formed,
but also why it is used and what stories it can tell.
